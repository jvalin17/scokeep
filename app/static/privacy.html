Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What Scokeep Is

Scokeep is a free score tracker for the Judgement (Kachuful) card game. It runs at scokeep.com.

Data We Collect

  • Player names — entered when you create a room. These are display names only (e.g., "Alice", "Bob"), not real identities.
  • Game data — bids, scores, hands won, round history, and game settings for each game played.
  • Room name and PIN — chosen by you to create and access your room. The PIN is hashed before storage.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No email addresses or real names
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts
  • No advertising cookies

How Data Is Stored

  • Server — game and room data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon. Session cookies are signed and expire after 30 days.
  • Your device — offline/Quick Game data is stored in IndexedDB (browser local storage) so games work without internet. This data stays on your device unless you sync it to a room.

Cookies

Scokeep uses a single session cookie (scokeep_session) to authenticate your room access. No third-party cookies are used.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties. There are no analytics services, ad networks, or external trackers.

How to Delete Your Data

  • Room data — use the "Clear Stats" button in the Stats screen to delete all game history for your room.
  • Local data — clear your browser's site data for scokeep.com to remove IndexedDB and cookies.
  • Full deletion — contact us and we will delete all data associated with your room name.

Children

Scokeep does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app collects only game scores and player nicknames, with no personal information required.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository or reach out through the app.
